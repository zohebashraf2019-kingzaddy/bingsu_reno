What is bingsu?

A bowl of Korean snow,
built in three layers.

  1. 01

    The snow 눈꽃

    Frozen milk is shaved into feather-light flakes that melt the moment they touch your spoon. Never crunchy, never icy.

  2. 02

    The toppings 토핑

    Fresh strawberries, ripe mango, cheesecake, chewy rice cakes, roasted soybean powder, sweet red bean — stacked by hand.

  3. 03

    The share 나눔

    Bingsu is made for sharing. Grab a couple of spoons, a friend, and dig in from the top down. Sweet moments, share happiness.

Good to know

Little questions.

How do you pronounce bingsu?

“Bing-soo” (빙수). It literally means “ice water” in Korean — but ours is much fluffier than that sounds.

Is one bowl enough to share?

Our bowls are generous, and bingsu is traditionally shared — two people per bowl is the sweet spot.

What is injeolmi?

Injeolmi (인절미) is a traditional Korean rice cake coated in roasted soybean powder. It gives bingsu a nutty, toasty, not-too-sweet flavour.

Where will the truck be this week?

Our regular spot is 10500 Stead Blvd in Reno, Wednesday to Saturday. Pop-up locations are announced on our Instagram and TikTok each week.

Can we book the truck for an event?

Send us a DM on Instagram with your date, location and guest count and we’ll get back to you.
